About Pandaisia Chocolates

Our Company

We are a company located in Essex, Vermont, dedicated to making delicious chocolate and other treats. For our founder, chocolate is more than food; it’s an art. This means using only the finest organic ingredients, incorporating a harmonious blend of rich flavors and smooth textures.

Anne learned her trade as part of a three-year apprenticeship program in Switzerland. Her introduction into the world of confectionary arts was shaped through working with leaders in the field. Early in 1993 she brought that expertise back to Vermont and Pandaisia Chocolates was born.
