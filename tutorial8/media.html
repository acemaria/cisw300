Maria's yoga routine

Yoga is a practice that combines movement, breathing, and mindfulness to help improve both physical and mental well-being. Many people use yoga to reduce stress, increase flexibility, and improve balance. It can be done almost anywhere and requires very little equipment.

My morning yoga routine focuses on simple poses that help me wake up and feel more energized. The poses in this video include Child's Pose, Cat-Cow, and Downward Dog. These poses help stretch the body, improve posture, and prepare me for the day ahead.

I enjoy practicing yoga because it helps me relax and clear my mind. Even spending a few minutes each day can make a big difference in how I feel physically and mentally.

Relaxing Music

One of my favorite songs right now is Happy Now by Kali Uchis. The song was released on her album Red Moon In Venus in 2023 and has a calm, dreamy sound that helps create a relaxing atmosphere.

I chose this song because it has a peaceful vibe that pairs well with my morning yoga routine. When I practice poses such as Child's Pose, Downward Dog, Pigeon Pose, and Seated Side Stretch, listening to calming music helps me focus on my breathing and stay present in the moment.

The soft instrumentals and smooth vocals make the song feel comforting and uplifting. It helps me start my day with a positive mindset and creates a relaxing environment while practicing yoga..
