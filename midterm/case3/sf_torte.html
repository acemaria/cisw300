french macarons

by lila

A macaron is a sweet, meringue-based French sandwich cookie made from egg whites, almond flour, and sugar. It features a crisp outer shell, a moist and chewy interior, and a flavorful filling like ganache, buttercream, or jam. French macarons are naturally gluten-free by ingredients and come in many colors and flavors, making them popular for gifts, celebrations, weddings, parties, and everyday treats..

Ingredients

  • shell: 100 g super‑fine blanched almond flour (about 1 cup).
  • 2100 g powdered (confectioners’) sugar (about 1 cup).
  • 75 g egg whites (from ~2 large eggs, room temperature).
  • 75 g fine granulated sugar (about ¾ cup).
  • 3¼ tsp cream of tartar.
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt.
  • 1–2 drops gel food coloring (optional).
  • for fillings: 1 cup buttercream or ganache (recipe of choice).

Directions

  1. Prep:Preheat oven to 250 °F (120 °C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper. Sift almond flour and powdered sugar together 3–4 times until smooth
  2. Whip egg whites:In a clean, dry bowl, beat egg whites on medium speed until foamy. Add granulated sugar 1 tbsp at a time, continuing to beat until stiff, glossy peaks form. Add cream of tartar and salt; beat until peaks hold firmly.
  3. Combine: Gradually add the sifted almond flour–sugar mixture to the meringue, beating until the batter thickens and forms a ribbon that slowly falls off the beaters
  4. Macarage:Fold the batter gently with a spatula until smooth and uniform in texture (no dry pockets). Rest the batter for 30 min to let the surface skin form
  5. Pipe & bake: Pipe 1½ in (38 mm) circles onto parchment. Bake at 250 °F for ~18 min until shells have risen and developed “feet.” Cool completely before filling.
  6. fill:Pipe or spread your chosen filling between two shells.
  7. Tips for success:Use super‑fine almond flour; coarse meal will affect texture. Ensure all tools are grease‑free before whipping egg whites. Resting time and gentle folding are key to smooth shells and proper “feet.” First batch may not be perfect — macarons improve with practice.
  8. Yield: ~20 filled macarons Storage: Keep in airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days; fill just before serving for best texture.
  9. This recipe uses cups for dry ingredients and teaspoons for small amounts, making it easy to measure with standard kitchen tools!!
